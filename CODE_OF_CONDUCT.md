# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in BLACK ICE respectful and constructive. Participants must act professionally, assume good faith where reasonable, critique ideas rather than people, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or intentionally disruptive behavior.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or interactions that violate this standard. Reports should be sent privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Please do not include credentials or private information in public reports.

This short policy applies to project issues, pull requests, code review, and other project spaces. It is not a substitute for the platform's terms or safety policies.
